name: Meeting brief
format: md
description: A short brief to read in the minutes before a meeting
required_sections:
  - Objective
  - Attendees
  - Our position
  - Key messages
  - Likely questions and responses
  - Red lines
  - Preparation checklist
style_guide: |-
  - Readable in under 5 minutes; if it runs past two pages, cut it.
  - Objective in one sentence: what a successful meeting looks like.
  - For each attendee: role, influence and what matters to them, from their stakeholder file.
  - Key messages in priority order, specific to this meeting, never generic.
  - Base likely questions and positions on documented sources; never invent a stance.
  - Red lines are explicit. Mark unknown dates or facts with [PLACEHOLDER: <what is needed> - <owner> - <date>].
tags: []
---

A one-page brief to walk into a meeting prepared: the goal, who is in the room and
what they care about, the points to land, the questions to expect, and what not to
agree to.
