style_guide: |-
  - One idea per slide. The slide title states the takeaway, not the topic.
  - Six lines per slide maximum. If it needs more, it needs another slide.
  - Structure with the pyramid principle: answer first, then the support.
  - Charts must carry a title that states the finding, not the measurement.
  - Close with a decisions-required slide naming each owner and date.

A slide deck for an executive or board audience. Built on the pyramid principle:
